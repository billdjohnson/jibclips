Why it works

One small part, one stubborn job

  • A dip, not a washer

    Cheap washers from overseas just squeeze. The Jibclip's dip cradles the charm's post and holds both the charm and the clip in place — no adhesive, nothing to wear out.

  • You won't feel it

    The clip rests inside the shoe on beveled edges, so there's no hard corner pressing against your foot.

  • Made here, by one person

    Designed and 3D printed in the USA by the person who thought of it — along with every photo, animation, and drawing on this page.

Installation

Listen for the snap

  1. Push the charm through the hole in your shoe, the same way you always have.
  2. Slide the Jibclip on from inside the shoe, under the base of the charm.
  3. Push until it snaps. That satisfying click is how you know it's seated.
If it doesn't click into place, it's not a Jibclip.

It's a tight fit on purpose — that grip is the whole point, and it can take a firm push. If you'd rather not muscle it on by hand, there's an optional install tool available on the listing. Taking one off is the reverse: push down on the charm, then slide the clip up and back off the post.

Will it fit my charms?

Jibclips work with any shoe charm that has a center post — you'll hear that snap when one seats. They fit genuine Crocs® and Jibbitz®; off-brand clogs can't be guaranteed, and they're not suited to shoes with a fleece or fabric lining. Not sure about yours?
